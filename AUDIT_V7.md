# V7 content, bilingual and Cartesian production audit

Source basis: **the user's ISACA/CMMI Model Quick Reference Guide v4.0 (2026)** and original educational case material. Public Practice Group summaries are not the underlying licensed normative practice statements.

## Audit conclusions

- 31 Practice Areas represented; sum of publicly reported practice counts: 276.
- PQA contains the six individual publicly exemplified practices with citations to pages 32–33.
- 30 remaining Practice Areas: **99 illustrated Practice Group learning cards** (per public PG level) with French and English explanation, contextual example, evidence and counterexample, checkpoint and page-specific official attribution.
- This is NOT a reconstruction of 270 individual licensed practice statements. Every card is explicitly labeled as an original interpretation of the corresponding public PG summary. No invented normative practice IDs.
- Completion of a PA now requires the PG (or PQA) checks in the chosen language as well as the separate 5-question 80% mastery test.
- Existing exam A/B banks kept separate from training questions, but no expert psychometric evaluation, blind testing or guarantee of exam pass rate.
- English/French technical explanations checked for fields, complete labels and distinct options. Content has NOT had an accredited bilingual instructor's full linguistic/technical signoff.

## Cartesian video audit

- 80 local video compositions and 80 VTT (40 lessons × FR/EN) generated with source-derived scenarios and steps.
- 5 specific pedagogical scenes per 35s composition, Cartesian palette and typographic rules; no `<audio>` elements, no prerecorded speech, browser TTS removed.
- A representative CAR scene was inspected locally in Chromium using an actual screenshot; CSS canvas height defect fixed before publishing.
- New HyperFrames workflow checks real MP4 audio-stream count is zero for every film and all 80 assets exist before committing to `main`.
- Renderer quality: `looks` at 18 fps. Rendering and final deployment status must be confirmed from GitHub Actions and Vercel before labeling all replacement MP4 published.

## Non-negotiable limitations

- The publicly available quick guide expressly states that it cannot substitute the full Model Viewer for appraisals or courses. Each of the remaining 270 individual practice statements and examples requires lawful model access, authorized usage and subject-matter review.
- A personal ISACA credential also requires its mandatory training/eligibility requirements. An organizational appraisal is a distinct process.
- Synthetic explanations and exam questions are independent; they are neither official past papers nor accredited assessment items.
